The coach application

The application to join the bench.

You are here because your interest earned a closer look. This is the deep application, and it is graded the way we would grade a hire onto staff, on evidence, against a bar you have cleared yourself. It asks for your real track record with proof, seven references, and a work sample. A live coaching session follows for every finalist. Give it a clear hour; it takes the work seriously, on purpose.

Foundry builds the founder before the company. A coach here is not measured by a certificate. They are measured by what they have built and whether they can develop a founder against an investor-grade standard.

1You

2Track record, with proof

Role by role, what you personally owned, not what the team did. Specifics are the point: numbers, timelines, your exact part. Vague claims score low, on purpose.

3Coaching and why

4Your place on the bench

Where your edge is, and where you could carry founders. This is your view; placement is decided on the evidence.

5The work sample

This is weighted heaviest, because the best predictor of whether someone can coach is watching them coach. This is the written part; a live session follows.
The situation. A Builder-stage founder opens your session upbeat: lots of inbound interest, a busy pipeline, and they want to start raising a round now. There is no revenue yet, and when you ask, they cannot name their one governing metric. In your own approach, how do you run this hour? What do you diagnose, what do you ask before you advise, and what single move do you leave them with by next week?

6Seven references

Spread across former bosses, peers, and people who reported to you. We ask each the same questions, including whether they would enthusiastically rehire you. Give people who have seen you operate up close.

7Consent and the standard

Ethan reviews every application himself, on the evidence. Submitting is a serious step, not an enquiry.
