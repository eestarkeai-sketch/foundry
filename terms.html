2. The Program

Starke Foundry is an identity-first founder program delivered over roughly 45 weeks across three stages — Seeker, Builder, and Sovereign (15 weeks each) — entered at the stage matching your proven capability. It includes a weekly curriculum, weekly one-hour coaching sessions with an assigned coach, weekly deliverables, stage gates, physical books shipped by stage, an evidence-based certificate, and a public registry of certified founders. We may reasonably modify the curriculum, coaches, schedule, and Program elements over time.

  • Weekly sessions occur once per week for 60 minutes. Rescheduling requires at least 24 hours’ notice; a late cancellation or no-show forfeits that week’s session with no make-up or credit.
