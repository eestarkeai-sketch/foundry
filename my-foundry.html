Pilot Guide
What the pilot tests, and what we ask of you
The pilot tests the whole path: applying and admission, paying, entering the Foundry, the weekly units, books and readings, submitting work, coaching, review, and getting help when something goes wrong. What we ask: use the Foundry normally, at your own pace through the weekly units. Report anything that blocks you, confuses you or looks wrong. Answer the short check-ins when they appear below (about a minute each; you can skip any). At the end there is a short exit review.
What it costs
$1 for each five-week Seeker block, charged when the block begins, for at most three blocks: $3 in total. The pilot covers the Seeker stage only. There is no Builder billing in the pilot, and nothing is charged after your third block.
Where your books and readings appear
Each unit under Your Program shows its reading. The printed books for each movement are ordered and shipped to you by the Foundry. A few later units have required readings that are not yet available in the pilot. Those units say the reading is held, and they cannot be submitted until it is available. That is expected, not a fault, but tell us if it stops you.
How coaching and review work
Your coach's messages appear under Messages with your coach. When you submit a unit, it is reviewed and the outcome appears on that unit. Your coaching, your submissions and your reviews are never attached to a support report.
Getting help, and what is urgent
Use Report a problem below. You get a reference number at once. Replies appear here under Your reports, and an email tells you when a reply is waiting. You can add to a report at any time. Urgent: if you were charged unexpectedly, cannot get in after paying, see something that looks like another person's information, or think a record of your progress or work is wrong, choose that category or tick the box. Urgent reports are looked at first. Never put card numbers or passwords in a report.
Leaving feedback
Short check-ins appear below at moments that matter: admission, paying, your first time in, your first unit, reading, submission, coaching and review, and when the pilot ends. Say what got in the way and what worked. Your feedback shapes what is reviewed after the pilot; it is kept apart from your developmental record.
