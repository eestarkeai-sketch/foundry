The gap

The market is crowded with resources and starved of the one thing that decides who makes it.

Most founders do not fail for lack of an idea or lack of capital. They fail for lack of the identity, judgment, and story to carry one through. Everyone will sell you the resource. Almost no one will build the founder.

Accelerators

Optimize the company and gate on capital. They assume a founder who is already formed, and turn away the ones who are not yet fundable.

Coworking

Sell space and proximity. Resource only. A desk has never once made a founder.

Masterminds

Sell peers and accountability. Peer only. No method, no curated expertise, no one developing the person.

Courses

Sell mechanics to anyone who pays. Content only. No selection, no identity, no transformation.

Most incubators make the company presentable.
Foundry makes the founder undeniable.

The fit

Foundry is not for everyone. On purpose.

Foundry is for you if

  • You are high agency, or ready to become so, and you move.
  • You are building for real, not collecting another program.
  • You are willing to be challenged, candidly, because you want the truth more than the comfort.
  • You believe the founder is the venture's most important asset. Starting with you.

Foundry is not for you if

  • You want only capital. An accelerator serves that.
  • You want only space. Coworking serves that.
  • You want only a network. A mastermind serves that.
  • You want to be told you are already finished. Foundry will not.
