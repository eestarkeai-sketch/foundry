The Coach Standard

What it means to coach at Starke Foundry.

Read this before you apply. If it isn’t you, don’t apply — and that’s a good outcome for both of us.

Foundry builds the founder before the company. Coaches are how that happens. You are not a cheerleader, a consultant, or a service vendor. You hold a founder against a bar and help them clear it on their own power.

Who we take

You have built or operated something real. You coach from scars, not theory. Credentials are welcome but never a substitute for having done the thing.

How you coach — the ten we hold you to

  • Identity first. You develop the founder, not just the deliverable. The person is the product.
  • Be socratic. Guide, don’t control. The founder makes the decision. You sharpen the thinking.
  • Tell the truth, even when it’s hard — and never destructively. Candor with care.
  • Hold the bar. You don’t lower the standard to be liked. Clearing the gate is the whole of finishing.
  • Show up on a rhythm. A reliable weekly session beats heroic ad-hoc availability. Consistency is the job.
  • Respond within one business day. Founders move; so do you.
  • Give first. You start by giving value. The paid tier is earned, not the reason you’re here.
  • Hold everything in confidence. What a founder shares stays with Foundry. Non-negotiable.
  • Disclose conflicts. Never solicit. No competing interests hidden; no taking founders, coaches, or business off Foundry. Ever.
  • Say “I don’t know” when you don’t. Then help them find who does.

How you rise

You start as a Guide (volunteer), coaching Seekers. You climb by contribution the system measures: hours you give, founders you carry through a stage, and coaches and founders you bring in. Quality is the multiplier — founders rate you confidentially at the end of each stage, and that rating moves you faster than volume ever will.

What we give you

A method that works, a roster matched to your lane, a workspace that carries the admin, a credential that means something, and — at the top — real compensation. Above all, the chance to build founders who build the future.
