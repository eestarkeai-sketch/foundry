How Foundry Works

The short version, so nothing surprises you later.

Foundry builds the founder before the company. It’s demanding by design — the pressure is where the building happens. Here’s exactly how the arc runs.

1The arc

Three stages — Seeker, Builder, Sovereign — over about 45 weeks, 15 weeks each. You enter at the stage where your proven capability actually ends, not where your ambition starts. Placement comes from your application, and it’s final once set.

2The weekly rhythm

A new week opens every Monday. Read the week’s material, do the deliverable, and bring it to your coaching session. Finish a week — view the material and submit the deliverable — to unlock the next one.

3The rules (and why)

The schedule is fixed and doesn’t pause. Missed weeks don’t roll over, and fees aren’t refundable. This isn’t strictness for its own sake — a real founder operates under a real clock, and the program mirrors that on purpose. If you want a course to dabble in, this isn’t it.

4Your coach

You’re matched with a vetted coach and meet one hour every week. That hour is for pressure-testing real work, not catching up on the reading — come prepared. Sessions are recorded and transcribed so you and your coach can focus on the conversation, not note-taking.

5Your books

Physical books ship to you by stage as part of the work. A few titles that aren’t published yet are provided as read-only readings inside the portal.

6The gates

You advance by clearing the bar, not by time served. Each stage ends with a Velocity Test measured against a clear rubric; Ethan reviews the evidence and signs off on every gate personally.

7The summit

Finish the arc and meet the standard, and you earn an evidence-based certificate signed by Ethan Starke and a place on the public registry of certified founders (you can opt out).

8Support & conduct

Message us any time in the portal — we reply within a business day. Treat coaches and fellow founders with respect, and keep what others share in confidence. That’s the whole code.
