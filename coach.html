Join the bench

Coach founders against a bar you have already cleared.

Starke Foundry builds the founder before the company. It is delivered by a hand-picked bench of operators and specialists, each oriented to one method and one standard. We are looking for a few more of the right people. If that is you, this is where you raise your hand.

What Foundry is

Foundry is an identity-first incubator. It does not make a company presentable; it builds the person the company depends on, over three stages and forty-five weeks, coached every week against an investor-grade standard. Founders are not coached by a personality. They are coached against a bar, by people who have cleared it themselves.

What a coach does

The unit

One hour a week

A single one-hour session per founder each week, on Zoom. A working session, not a lecture. Every week ends in a real move the founder owes by the next.

The method

Teach to the week

You carry the founder through the method: the week's marker, the teaching that translates it to their real venture, and the one defined move you assign and hold them to.

The bench

Continuous, not solo

Foundry is a bench, not a single voice. After each session you leave a short handoff, so any coach can pick up a founder and give the same read.

The contract

Candor with care

Tell the hard truth without cruelty and without flattery. Pushback is delivered as an optimization puzzle. Agency stays with the founder; you advise and challenge, you do not run their company.

Who we are looking for

Operators who have built real things and can develop founders against a standard they have met themselves. Ethan carries identity, narrative, leadership judgment, and the gates; the bench carries specialist depth. If your edge is one of these, we want to hear from you.

  • Finance and capital. Instruments and terms, cap tables, the raise, growth capital.
  • Legal and structure. Entity, governance, the terrain a founder must own.
  • Product and zero to one. Discovery, taste, velocity, the build standard.
  • Go to market and revenue. Channels, pricing, speed to revenue, enterprise deals.
  • Media, narrative, and presence. The founder's voice, the room, the story that raises.

How it works from here

This is an expression of interest, not a formal application. If there is a fit, Ethan follows up personally, and a formal application and a conversation come next. We keep the bench small and deliberate, so we move carefully rather than quickly.
