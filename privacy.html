Privacy Policy
Effective August 2026

This Policy explains what personal information Starke Foundry (“Foundry,” “we,” operated by Ethan Starke) collects, why, how we handle it, and your choices. It is incorporated into our Terms of Service. Contact: ethan@ethanstarke.com.

1. Information we collect

  • Application information: your name, email, phone, professional link, self-assessment answers, essays, and an application video link.
  • Identity verification: to confirm you are a real, eligible person, we use a third-party identity-verification provider, which processes government-ID and related data under its own terms; we receive a verification result and limited associated data.
  • Enrollment & billing: shipping address (for book fulfillment) and payment information. Card details are processed by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Program participation: your stage, progress, submitted weekly deliverables, coach notes about you, and recordings and automatically generated transcripts of your coaching sessions.
  • Communications: messages you send us or your coach, and support requests.
  • Site usage: limited, privacy-friendly analytics (see Cookies below).

2. How we use it

To review applications and verify identity; to place, enroll, and bill you; to deliver the Program (coaching, curriculum, gates, certificate, registry); to ship your books; to support you; to operate, secure, and improve the Program; to send Program and transactional emails; and to comply with law.

3. Service providers

We share personal information with providers who process it on our behalf, including our website and account platform, our book-fulfillment retailer, our video-session and transcription provider, our identity-verification provider, and our privacy-friendly analytics provider. We require providers to protect your information and use it only to provide their service. We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, users, or others.

4. Session recordings & transcripts

Coaching sessions are recorded and transcribed automatically through our video-session provider. Recordings and transcripts are accessible to you, your assigned coach, and us, and support coaching, your recall, and quality review (including AI-assisted review). You consent to recording and transcription at enrollment and via an in-session notice.

5. Data retention

We generally retain your information for as long as needed to operate the Program and our business, including records related to your participation, certificate, and registry, and we may retain information (including declined applications and session transcripts) on an ongoing basis for these purposes. We will delete or de-identify information on request as described below, subject to legitimate business and legal retention needs.

6. Your choices and rights

You may request access to, a copy of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting ethan@ethanstarke.com; we verify your identity and respond within a reasonable period consistent with applicable law. You may opt out of the public registry at any time. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under applicable state privacy laws.

7. Cookies & analytics

We use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics to understand how our site is used. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. If this changes, we will provide a consent mechanism.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including access controls (for example, application essays are restricted to us, and coaches see only the information needed to coach their assigned founders). No system is perfectly secure. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected users without undue delay, consistent with applicable law.

9. Children

The Program is for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy; material changes will be communicated and the effective date updated.
