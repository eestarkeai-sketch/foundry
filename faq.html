Frequently Asked Questions
What is Starke Foundry?

An identity-first founder program: we build the founder before the company, across three stages — Seeker, Builder, Sovereign — over about 45 weeks, with weekly coaching and a curriculum you complete week by week.

How do I get in?

Apply — it’s free. We read every application ourselves and reply within three business days. Placement is by proven capability, not ambition; we place you at the stage where your demonstrated capability actually ends.

What does it cost?

Billed in five-week blocks by stage: Seeker $1,250, Builder $2,500, Sovereign $3,750 per block. A discount applies if you pay a full stage upfront. All prices are in U.S. dollars.

How much time does it take?

Plan on real weekly work plus a one-hour coaching session, every week, for the length of your arc.

Is it flexible?

No, deliberately. The schedule is fixed and doesn’t pause, missed weeks don’t roll over, and fees are non-refundable. Foundry is built for people who want that pressure, not a course to dabble in.

Do you guarantee results?

No. We guarantee a standard and a process. Your outcomes are yours and depend on your own effort and circumstances.

Who coaches me?

A vetted coach matched to your work. You help choose from matched options; we approve the match.

Do I get the books?

Yes — physical books ship to you by stage as part of the work. A few not-yet-published titles are provided as read-only readings in the portal.

What do I get at the end?

An evidence-based certificate signed by Ethan Starke, and a place on the public registry of certified founders (you can opt out).

Where can I read the fine print?

See our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Questions: ethan@ethanstarke.com.
